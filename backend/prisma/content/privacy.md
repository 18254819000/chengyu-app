# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 20, 2026**

Daily Chengyu ("the App") is a Chinese idiom learning app operated by **Tai'an Mengma Software Service Co., Ltd.** ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does and does not collect, and how any information involved in operating the App is handled. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

The short version: **the App does not require an account, and we do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.**

More specifically:

- **No account registration.** The App can be used without signing up or logging in. We never ask you to create a profile.
- **Learning data stays on your device.** Your quiz results, best scores, reading position, and app preferences are stored locally on your device only. They are **not** uploaded to our servers.
- **Subscription records.** If you subscribe, payment is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. To verify your subscription and unlock paid content, our server stores your subscription's **original transaction identifier** and its entitlement status (such as active, expired, or refunded), as provided by Apple. This identifier is tied to your Apple ID transaction, not to any name or contact information held by us.
- **Standard server logs.** When the App fetches content (for example, the idiom list or the daily idiom), our cloud hosting providers record routine technical logs, which may include your IP address, device model, operating system version, and request time. These logs are generated automatically by the hosting platform and are used only for operating, securing, and troubleshooting the service.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

- We do not collect your contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, health data, or precise location.
- We do not use any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs in the App.
- We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or rent any data to third parties.

## 3. How We Use Information

The limited information described above is used only to:

- Deliver idiom content and daily updates to the App;
- Verify App Store subscriptions and unlock the content you have paid for;
- Keep the service secure, diagnose errors, and prevent abuse.

We do not use this information for advertising or marketing profiling.

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

Subscription records are stored on our servers (hosted by third-party cloud providers) for as long as needed to provide your subscription benefits and to comply with accounting and legal requirements. Server logs are retained by our hosting providers for a limited period in accordance with their policies. Data stored locally on your device remains there until you delete the App or clear its data.

## 5. Third Parties

- **Apple.** Purchases, subscriptions, renewals, and refunds are handled by Apple under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. We receive subscription status notifications from Apple so the App can reflect your current entitlement.
- **Cloud hosting providers.** Our backend runs on third-party cloud infrastructure, which processes the technical data described in Section 1 as part of providing the service.

We do not share information with any other third parties except where required by law.

## 6. Your Choices

- You can use the free content of the App without providing any information to us.
- You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your Apple ID account settings.
- Deleting the App removes all locally stored learning data from your device.
- If you have questions about the subscription records we hold, you can contact us at the email below.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App offers general-audience educational content. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App has no account system and collects no personal information from any user, no special children's data is gathered. Purchases can only be made through an Apple ID, which is controlled by the account holder.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when features change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date, and the changes take effect when posted. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
